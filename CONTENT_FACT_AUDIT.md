## Editorial rule adopted

For future song pages:
1. Keep factual metadata separate from interpretation.
2. Do not state recording years, historical claims, or attribution details as fact unless sourced.
3. Avoid unsupported superlatives such as «یکی از ماندگارترین»، «درخشان‌ترین»، or «قشنگ‌ترین» unless clearly framed as opinion or supported by an attributable source.
4. Keep composer/lyricist naming normalized with Persian ZWNJ.
5. When a title has a documented alternate title, consider storing it explicitly instead of relying only on prose.
